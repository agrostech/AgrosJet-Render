**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**

*   **Roles & Dashboards:** System Manager, Super Admin, Admin, Courier.
*   **Core Functionality:**
    *   **Courier Management:** Independent registration, search by phone, adding to a company roster, viewing details.
    *   **Company & Admin Management:** System Manager manages companies and Super Admins. Super Admins manage Admins and Couriers.
    *   **Shift Management (Vardiya Yönetimi):** A grid-based system where admins can create shifts (e.g., 11:00-23:00) and assign couriers to them for each day of the week.     *   **Current Status (Güncel Durum):** A dashboard overview showing the current day's shift report. It highlights the active shift, shows how many couriers are in each shift, and lists the assigned couriers in a compact, multi-line grid.
    *   **Accounting (Muhasebe):** A system with three sub-tabs (Couriers, Businesses, Vendors). It allows tracking balances by adding Payment In (tahsilat) and Payment Out (borçlandırma) transactions. For couriers, a Hakediş (Payroll) checkbox is available when recording a payment. Businesses and Vendors (Cariler) can be created and managed within their respective tabs.
    *   **Assigned Items (Zimmet):** Placeholder for future implementation.
*   **UI/UX & Design:**
    *   Professional, text-heavy, responsive design with Turkish character support.
    *   Collapsible left-side navigation menu that is fixed and does not scroll with page content.
    *   Data tables should be clean, using zebra-striping and clear borders.
