<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
The user wants to build a comprehensive courier management system from scratch called SHIFTJET KURYE YÖNETİM SİSTEMİ.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**

*   **Roles & Dashboards:** System Manager, Super Admin, Admin, Courier.
*   **Core Functionality:**
    *   **Courier & Company Management:** Full CRUD operations for companies, admins, and couriers with role-based restrictions.
    *   **Profile Management:** Users can manage their own credentials.
    *   **Shift Management (Vardiya Yönetimi):** A grid-based system for assigning couriers to shifts.
    *   **Current Status (Güncel Durum):** A dashboard overview of the daily shift report.
    *   **Accounting (Muhasebe):** A multi-tab module for tracking financial transactions, including PDF export and search functionality. A bulk payroll entry feature is on hold.
    *   **Activity Logs (Hareketler):** A log of all major financial and system operations.
    *   **Assigned Items (Zimmet):** A system to track items assigned to couriers.
    *   **Installment Products (Taksitli Ürün):** A system within accounting to track and pay for products in installments for couriers.
    *   **System Page:** A new section for future system-wide settings.
    *   **Courier Dashboard:** A read-only dashboard for couriers to view their own shifts, accounting, and assigned items.
    *   **Invoice Management:** A system for couriers to upload invoices for payroll transactions, and for admins to manage, review, and download them.
    *   **Termination Process:** A feature for admins to initiate a formal termination period for couriers.
    *   **Document Management (Evraklar):** A new section for couriers to upload required documents (contract, ID, license, etc.) and for admins to review, manage, and download them.
    *   **Loyalty Points System (JetPuan Market):** A points-based e-commerce system where couriers earn points from payroll and spend them on products. Admins manage the store.
    *   **Notification System (Bildirimler):** A real-time notification system for admins for critical events, complete with a UI popover and sound alerts.

**User's preferred language**: Turkish
